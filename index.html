Design inspiration: LinaHua (@Linahuaa)

Her concept: a solo creator, empowered by modern tools, making a Final Fantasy VI × Fate/Stay Night hybrid — AAA anime stills, kickass dialogue, goated characters, and battle mechanics that punish spam in favor of multi-step setups.

It may take ten years, and nobody may play it. It will still be a personal favourite.

World, systems, and vertical slice implemented as a browser-native tribute to that vision. No engine but Canvas, localStorage, and the Web Audio API.
