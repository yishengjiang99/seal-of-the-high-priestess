Common questions

  • How do I play? Use the d-pad to move. Z confirms or talks, X cancels or runs, ☰ opens the menu, and ⛺ rests at a lotus altar. A game controller or Bluetooth keyboard also works (WASD or arrows, Z, X, Esc).
  • How do I save? Open the menu and choose Save, or rest at a lotus altar. The game also makes a suspend save when you leave the app while exploring, and Continue picks up from your latest save.
  • Is my progress backed up? Yes. When you're online, saves sync to our server and follow your iCloud Keychain to a new iPhone. If two devices changed the same slot, the app asks which copy to keep. The other copy is kept in history.
  • Can I play offline? Yes. The whole game, including voices, is inside the app. Sync catches up later.
  • No sound? Check the silent switch, or turn on Play sound in silent mode in Settings. You can also check Options → Master Volume / Voice Volume.
  • Delete my data? Settings → Delete cloud data. See the Privacy Policy.
