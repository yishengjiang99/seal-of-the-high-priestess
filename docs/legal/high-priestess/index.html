Temple of the High Priestess

A JRPG of purification and poisoned words · iPhone · Free to start · GrepAwk LLC

Elara is seventeen, the High Priestess of the Silver Lotus, and the only thing holding the demon prince Kael in his chains. Together they cross a poisoned land in slow, deliberate battles where spending Mana, meditating and breaking the High Seal matter more than grinding, and in cinematic, voiced scenes where every choice has weight.

  • Story-first JRPG / visual novel with thousands of words of original dialogue and fully voiced characters
  • Strategic battles: Mana spend, Meditate, charge-up turns, and the risky choice to unseal Kael
  • Named gear and techniques, no XP grind. Optional quests, lotus-altar camps, and a Temple to rebuild
  • Plays offline. Progress backs up and follows you to a new iPhone
  • Free prologue and first region; unlock the full game once. No ads, no subscriptions, nothing to grind for
