Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-07 · Product: Temple of the High Priestess (iPhone, iOS 17+) · Publisher: GrepAwk LLC

Temple of the High Priestess is a single-player story RPG. It has no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, no account sign-up and no in-app purchases. This policy explains the small amount of data the app sends to our server so your progress is backed up and can follow you to a new iPhone.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone audio, browsing history or purchase information. We do not use your data for tracking or advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Game content downloads

On launch the app may download updated game content (story text and balance numbers) from grepawk.com. That request includes only the app's built-in content version, not your player ID. As with any web request, our server sees your IP address in standard, short-lived server logs.

Where it is stored and for how long

Progress is stored on our server at grepawk.com (DigitalOcean, United States) for as long as you keep playing. The server also keeps the 10 most recent earlier versions of each save slot so a mistaken sync can be undone. A copy also stays on your iPhone.

Your choices

  • Delete your cloud data: in the app, open Settings and tap Delete cloud data. This permanently removes your player ID, saves, save history and settings from our server. Saves on your iPhone stay on your iPhone.
  • Play without syncing: turn off Cloud sync in the app's Settings. The game is fully playable offline.
  • You can also email yisheng.jiang@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your data. Include your player ID, which is shown in the app's Settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the only data it collects is the anonymous progress data described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
