Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-07 · Updated: 2026-10-07 (in-app purchases) · Product: Temple of the High Priestess (iPhone, iOS 17+) · Publisher: GrepAwk LLC

Temple of the High Priestess is a single-player story RPG. It has no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs and no account sign-up. The prologue and first region are free; the rest of the story is a one-time in-app purchase, and there is an optional cosmetic Supporter Pack. This policy explains the small amount of data the app sends to our own server so your progress is backed up, your purchases unlock on all your devices, and we can see whether the free part of the game and the unlock screen are working.

Game content downloads

On launch the app may download updated game content (story text, balance numbers, the wording of the unlock screen and feature switches) from grepawk.com. The content is digitally signed and only ever contains data, never code. That request includes only the app's built-in content version, not your player ID. As with any web request, our server sees your IP address in standard, short-lived server logs.

Where it is stored and for how long

Progress, purchase records and events are stored on our server at grepawk.com (DigitalOcean, United States) for as long as you keep playing; events older than 13 months are deleted. The server also keeps the 10 most recent earlier versions of each save slot so a mistaken sync can be undone. A copy also stays on your iPhone.

Your choices

  • Delete your cloud data: in the app, open Settings and tap Delete cloud data. This permanently removes your player ID, saves, save history, settings, purchase records and events from our server. Saves on your iPhone stay on your iPhone, and your purchases are still yours: tap Restore Purchases to unlock them again.
  • Play without syncing: turn off Cloud sync in the app's Settings. The game is fully playable offline.
  • You can also email yisheng.jiang@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your data. Include your player ID, which is shown in the app's Settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the only data it collects is the pseudonymous progress, purchase and event data described above. Purchases by children are subject to Apple's parental controls (Ask to Buy).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
